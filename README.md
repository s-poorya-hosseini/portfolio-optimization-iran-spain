# Portfolio Optimization: Iran & Spain Markets

This repository presents a computational finance research project developed as part of a Master's thesis in Economics at Tarbiat Modares University.

The project evaluates the performance of classical portfolio optimization models and machine-learning-assisted allocation frameworks across two heterogeneous financial markets:
- Tehran Stock Exchange (TEDPIX)
- Spanish Equity Market (IBEX 35)

## Research Objective

The primary objective of this research was to examine whether machine-learning-assisted portfolio optimization approaches consistently outperform classical financial optimization models across different market structures.

## Methodological Framework

The project integrates:

- Classical Markowitz optimization
  - Minimum Variance Portfolio
  - Maximum Sharpe Ratio Portfolio

- Machine-learning-assisted allocation framework
  - Ridge Regression
  - Mixed-Integer Programming (MIP)

- Online Portfolio Selection (OLPS) models
  - CRP
  - EG
  - OLMAR

## Data Categories

The empirical framework incorporates multiple categories of financial information:

- Macroeconomic variables
  - Interest rates
  - Oil prices
  - Gold prices
  - S&P 500
  - TEDPIX
  - IBEX 35

- Technical indicators
  - MACD
  - RSI
  - Moving Averages

- Volatility-based measures

## Research Environment

- Python-based computational research pipeline
- Rolling-window backtesting
- Portfolio rebalancing
- Comparative market analysis
- Reproducible quantitative workflows

## Important Note

This repository is intended to present the research structure and methodological framework of the project.

The complete thesis codebase and full datasets are not publicly available due to academic and institutional restrictions.
